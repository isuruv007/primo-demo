Before you ask

Good questions.

How does subscribe & save work?

Every 4 weeks we would send the same box at 15% off. Skip, swap flavours or cancel from your account. In this demo, nothing is ever sent.

When does shipping become free?

Orders of $60 or more after discounts ship free. Below that, standard shipping is $9.95, and the cart shows how close you are.

Can I mix flavours in a pack?

Not yet: each pack is one flavour. Add a different pack per flavour; the cart keeps them separate.

Is the bottle recyclable?

The bottle is made from recycled PET and the cap is HDPE, both kerbside recyclable in Australia. Cap on.

Can I actually buy PRIMO?

No. PRIMO is a fictional brand made for a design demo. The checkout charges nothing and sends nothing.

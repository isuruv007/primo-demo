Our story

Started on a kitchen bench.

Every Saturday our run club finished at the same corner store, staring at the same fridge. Neon drinks with a day's sugar in them, or electrolyte tablets that tasted like a swimming pool. Nothing we'd actually want to drink twice.

So we started mixing. Forty-one versions of the first lime, scribbled on a whiteboard, tested on anyone who'd stand still. The rule was simple: real electrolytes, zero sugar, and it has to taste good enough to drink when you're not thirsty.

“If it isn't good enough to drink on the couch, it isn't good enough for mile twenty.” The whiteboard, version 41

Version 41 became Volt Lime. Three more flavours followed, each built around a part of the day: early runs, long desk hours, late sessions, the gym after work.

What we won't compromise on

Four rules on the wall.

01

Zero sugar. No asterisk.

No sugar, no sugar alcohols that wreck your stomach at kilometre ten. Sweetened with stevia leaf and monk fruit.

02

Dose it properly

830 mg of electrolytes because that's what the research supports for real sweat losses, not because it looks good on a label.

03

Leave nothing behind

Every bottle is 100% recycled PET, every label peels off clean, and every empty has somewhere to go back to.

04

Say it plainly

No miracle claims. If a number is on our bottle, the source is on our site.
